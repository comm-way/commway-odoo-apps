EU Directive 2023/2673

Website Order Withdrawal

Helps you implement the online withdrawal function ("withdrawal button") required by Art. 11a of EU Directive 2023/2673, in Austria § 13a FAGG. Customers open the form via "Withdraw from contract", fill in their details and send their declaration with "Confirm withdrawal", directly in your Odoo website.

Frontend Form with Dynamic Validation & Privacy Compliance

Integrates a custom legal portal subpath (/shop/revocation) accessible anywhere.

Customers enter their name, e-mail address and order or contract number. An optional field lets them withdraw only part of an order (e.g. by item number); if it is left empty, the withdrawal covers the entire order. The declaration is sent with a clearly labelled "Confirm withdrawal" button. Registered customers are recognised automatically, while guests can submit a withdrawal without logging in.

Built-in Bot & Spam Protection

Public forms attract bots. Without protection, automated scripts can flood your backend with fake revocations and abuse the automatic receipt email to send messages to arbitrary addresses through your mail server, which can damage your domain's sender reputation. The withdrawal form is protected on two levels:

  • Cloudflare Turnstile / Google reCAPTCHA: The form uses Odoo's native captcha verification. If you have Turnstile (or reCAPTCHA) configured for your website, the challenge is shown automatically and every submission is verified server-side before a record is created or an email is sent.
  • Invisible honeypot: A hidden trap field that only bots fill in. Such submissions are silently discarded, with no record and no email, and this works even without a captcha configured.

💡 Recommended setup: Enable Cloudflare Turnstile under Website › Configuration › Settings and enter your site and secret key. No further configuration of this module is required.

Immediate Confirmation & Reference Tracking

Upon submission, the customer receives an instant on-screen confirmation with a unique reference number. At the same time, a receipt confirmation is sent by e-mail. It contains the content of the withdrawal declaration (name, e-mail, order, withdrawn items, reason) as well as the date and time of receipt.

Native Odoo Settings Integration

Configure the entire module directly from the core Sales app settings. Assign a dedicated Notification User to ensure new revocation requests are automatically routed to the correct agent's dashboard. You can also set the Privacy Policy URL and choose how the privacy policy is shown on the form: as a notice with link (default, no extra step for the customer) or as a mandatory checkbox.

Automated Task Assignment & Complete Chatter History

Every incoming revocation creates a structured record in the backend and automatically schedules an activity (Next Activity) for the designated Notification User. All automated email notifications and customer replies are directly tracked within the record's chatter, ensuring complete transparency and streamlined communication.

Centralized Management & Processing Pipeline

Keep your customer service team efficient with a dedicated list view directly within the Sales app. All incoming revocations are neatly organized, allowing you to filter by status (Received, In Review, Accepted, Rejected), search by reference, and process requests in bulk. The clear overview helps your team keep track of open requests and deadlines.

Comprehensive Detail View & Action Management

Inside the detail view, your team has all necessary customer data, order references, and submitted reasons available at a glance. Easily transition the status through the integrated progress bar and utilize the smart action buttons to instantly accept or reject the revocation, triggering the appropriate automated email workflows directly from the interface.

💡 Smart Collaboration: When you assign the revocation request to an internal user or link it to a specific customer record, Odoo automatically adds them as a Follower to the document thread. This ensures that any subsequent updates, comments, or customer replies in the chatter are seamlessly routed to their personal inbox.

Release Notes

Version 1.2.0 (Withdrawal button according to Art. 11a / § 13a FAGG, update recommended)

  • Changed: The submit button is now labelled "Confirm withdrawal" / "Widerruf bestätigen", as worded in the directive.
  • New: Partial withdrawal. Customers can specify which items they want to withdraw (empty = entire order). The items are shown in the backend record and in the receipt confirmation.
  • Improved: The receipt confirmation e-mail now contains the full content of the declaration (name, e-mail, order, items, reason) and the date and time of receipt, in English and German.
  • Improved: The order number is stored exactly as entered; unknown order numbers are highlighted in the backend.
  • Changed: Neutral introduction text on the form.
  • New: Setting "Privacy Policy Display". By default, the form now shows a privacy notice with a link instead of a mandatory checkbox, so that customers do not face an extra step. The checkbox can be re-enabled in the Sales settings.

Note when upgrading: The receipt confirmation e-mail template is updated automatically. Any manual changes you made to this template will be replaced.

Version 1.1.0 (Security update)

  • New: Server-side Cloudflare Turnstile / Google reCAPTCHA verification for the withdrawal form.
  • New: Invisible honeypot field that silently discards bot submissions.
  • Improved: Form keeps entered values and shows a clear message if the security check fails.

Upgrading from an older version: Download the new version, replace the module in your addons path and upgrade it under Apps. Then enable Cloudflare Turnstile in your website settings for full protection. We also recommend reviewing existing revocation records for automated spam entries.
